The problem: too much of one dish, too little of another

Attendance changes by weekday, by exam week and by menu. Preparation usually follows last week's habit. The gap shows up as leftovers on some items and stock-outs on others.

What FreshRoute does about it

It turns your own history into a forecast per dish and meal, explains where the number came from, and refuses to claim accuracy it has not measured.

Sustainability you can measure, not slogans

FreshRoute reports what your records show: quantities prepared, consumed and left over, in your own units. It does not convert food into carbon, money or meals saved unless you provide the cost per unit, and then it labels the result an estimate.
